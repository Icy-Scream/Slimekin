Slimekin: help

Raise a tiny slime. Boop it, feed it, pet it, and watch it grow.

Need a hand?

Write to jpetta@pixelbrew.org. Tell us what happened, which iPhone you have, and, if you can, add a screenshot.

Where is my slime saved?

On your phone. The game saves by itself every few seconds and when you leave it, and keeps a second copy on the phone in case iOS tidies up storage. Nothing is stored online.

Moving to a new phone

On the old phone open Settings (the gear) and tap Copy save code. Send the code to yourself in a way you trust. On the new phone open Settings, tap Load a save code, paste it and tap Load.

If you delete the app, the slime is deleted too. Copy a save code first if you want to keep it.

No sound?

Tap the screen once: phones only start a game's sound after a tap. Check that the volume is up and that sound is on in the game's Settings. If your phone is on silent, the game may stay quiet too.

Is it safe for kids?

Slimekin is a gentle game for everyone. There is no chat, no accounts, no ads, and nothing is shared with other players. Nothing bad happens to your slime while you are away.

Privacy

Slimekin collects nothing. The details are on the privacy page.
